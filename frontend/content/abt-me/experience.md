I recently finished my Master's in Computer and Information Science (With [tooltip content="yeah about 80% of the graduates ended up with first class honours idk what there is to brag about"]First Class Honours[/tooltip]!) at AUT, where my thesis was about an LLM-driven data pipeline management framework based on Apache Airflow, designed to work with less or non-technical staffs. 

I'm currently taking an intern position at a local medical assistive equipment company. I'm working with the Customer Care team to create an end-to-end AI-driven purchase order processing system based on Microsoft Azure cloud services. The system automatically retrieves incoming order emails and PDF attachments, uses OCR and AI to extract key details, and converts them into structured order data. This data is then validated, classified into different types of order, and then integrated into the company's ERP system. The goal is to reduce manual effort for simple, repetitive orders, allowing the Customer Care team to focus on more important, complicated cases.
