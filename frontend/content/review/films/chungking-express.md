A film about [tooltip content="Two people can share a room and still be a city apart."]proximity[/tooltip],
told twice, by a director who clearly did not plan the second half when he shot the first.

That should be a flaw. It isn't.

> We're all unlucky in love sometimes. When I am, I go jogging.

The second story is the one people quote, but the first is the one that stays with me —
mostly for what happens with [spoiler]the expiring cans of pineapple[/spoiler], which is
the most precise thing anyone has filmed about being dumped.

Shot in [tooltip content="A demolished labyrinth of a building in Tsim Sha Tsui. Most of the film's first half was shot inside it."]Chungking Mansions[/tooltip]
on almost no budget, and it looks better than films with a hundred times the money.

<!-- zh -->
一部关于[tooltip content="两个人可以共处一室，却隔着一整座城。"]靠近[/tooltip]的电影，讲了两遍。
导演拍前半段的时候显然还没想好后半段。

这本该是缺点，但它不是。

> 每个人都有失恋的时候，而我一失恋，就会去跑步。

大家爱引用的是第二个故事，但留在我心里的是第一个——主要因为[spoiler]那些会过期的凤梨罐头[/spoiler]，
那是我见过对"被甩"这件事最精确的影像表达。

在[tooltip content="尖沙咀一座迷宫般的大楼，影片前半段几乎都在里面拍摄。"]重庆大厦[/tooltip]里
几乎零预算拍出来的，比很多一百倍投资的片子都好看。
