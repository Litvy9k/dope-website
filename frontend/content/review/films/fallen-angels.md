The offcuts of Chungking Express, shot on the widest lenses available and pointed
at the ceiling for no reason anyone has satisfactorily explained.

Less coherent than its sibling, and I keep going back to it anyway.

<!-- zh -->
《重庆森林》剩下的边角料，用能找到的最广的镜头拍，还老是仰着拍天花板，
至今没人给出过令人信服的解释。

比它的姊妹篇散得多，但我还是一遍遍回去看。
