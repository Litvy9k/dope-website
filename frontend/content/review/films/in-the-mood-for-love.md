Two people discover their spouses are having an affair, and respond by rehearsing
the conversation they will never have. The whole film is people not saying things,
shot in corridors too narrow for two.

It is the most restrained film I can think of that is also unbearably horny.

两个人发现各自的配偶有染，于是开始排练一场永远不会发生的对话。
整部片子都是人在把话咽回去，拍在容不下两个人并行的走廊里。

我想不出还有哪部电影这么克制，又这么色情。
